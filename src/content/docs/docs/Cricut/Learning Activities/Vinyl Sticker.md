---
title: Vinyl Sticker
---

Cut a vinyl star decal sized for a mug. This is the shortest way to get comfortable with the whole Cricut workflow — design, mat, blade, cut — before you try something you care about.

**Read this first:** the [Cricut (Explore 4) manual](/docs/cricut/cricut-explore-4/). It covers the machine's controls, loading the mat, and what to do if something goes wrong.

## 1. Set up Design Space

Open **Cricut Design Space** on the lab computer, or install it on your own laptop by following [Setting up Design Space](/docs/cricut/cricut-explore-4/#setting-up-design-space) in the manual.

## 2. Create the design

1. Select **New Project**.
2. Choose **Vinyl Decal** from the project-type menu.
3. Choose the size for a **mug**, then click **Create**.
4. Open **Shapes** in the sidebar and place a **star** on the canvas.
5. Click **Make** in the top right, and save the project when prompted.

## 3. Set up the cut

1. Choose **On Mat** when asked how your material loads, and select the **12 in × 12 in** mat size.

   Use a mat for this exercise even if you have Smart Materials — the point is to learn the mat.

2. Peel the clear plastic film off the mat and set it aside; you'll put it back at the end. Press the vinyl onto the mat.
3. Check that there's actually vinyl underneath where the star sits in Design Space's mat preview. If there isn't, drag the star to a spot on the preview that matches where your vinyl is.
4. Click **Continue** and select your vinyl in **Browse All Materials**.
5. When Design Space asks you to load tools, open **Clamp B** and seat the fine-point blade. If the blade is already in place, open and close the clamp without changing anything.

   ![Clamp A and Clamp B closed, with the blade housing seated in Clamp B](../../assets/images/the_fab_lab_cricut_o_0c722af3ea.jpeg) ![Clamp B open with the blade housing removed](../../assets/images/the_fab_lab_cricut_o_43aa47aac4.jpeg)

6. Power on the Cricut and hold the mat flat against the material guides, up against the rollers. Press the **Load button** and keep holding the mat until the rollers pull it in.

## 4. Cut

Press the **Go button** once it starts flashing. Stay at the machine while it cuts.

When it's done, press **Load/Unload** to release the mat, peel the vinyl off the mat, and weed out the waste vinyl around the star. Put the plastic film back on the mat.

## 5. Clean up

Follow [Finishing up](/docs/cricut/cricut-explore-4/#finishing-up) in the manual: return the mat and material, clear your scraps, and turn the machine off.
