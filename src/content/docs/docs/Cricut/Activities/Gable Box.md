---
title: Gable Box
---

Build a gable box out of cardstock — the handled, ridge-topped box you get party favors in. It's a step up from the [Vinyl Sticker](/docs/cricut/activities/vinyl-sticker/) activity: you'll import someone else's design file, edit it in Design Space, and cut score lines and outlines in the same job.

**Read this first:** the [Cricut (Explore 4) manual](/docs/cricut/cricut-explore-4/).

## 1. Download the template

Go to [Free Large Gable Box Template SVG](https://sunnysvg.com/free-gable-box-templates/large-gable-box-svg/), scroll to the download section, click the blue **click here to download** button, and unzip the files.

## 2. Upload it to Design Space

1. Open Design Space and choose the **Cardstock Cutout** project type.

   ![Design Space home screen showing project types including Cardstock Cutout](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_8ec35a5b85.png)

2. Choose **Custom Size**, set the design area to **11.7 in × 12 in**, and click **Create**.

   ![The Select a project type dialog with Custom Size and a design area of 11.7 by 12 inches](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_114604843b.png)

3. Click **Upload** in the sidebar, then **Upload Image** under the **Image** tab.

   ![The Upload panel with the Upload Image button under the Image tab](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_e3b8082250.png)

4. Pick the **part 1** SVG from inside the unzipped folder. The template ships as two identical parts — you only need one, because you'll cut it twice.

   ![A file picker open on the template's SVG folder, showing the part 1 and part 2 files](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_c5e4fae03c.png)

5. Design Space previews the box outline. Click **Continue**.

   ![The Upload Image preview showing the gable box outline, with a Continue button](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_bd2e7c0bd4.png)

6. Leave the image name and tags alone and click **Upload**.

   ![The Image Details screen naming the file Gable Box part 1, with an Upload button](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_d231516149.png)

7. Add the uploaded image to your canvas. It should look like this:

   ![The gable box template placed on the Design Space canvas](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_ae8373a991.png)

## 3. Close the bottom of the box

The template is open at the bottom. You'll weld two rectangles over the bottom panels so the box has a floor.

1. Using the **rectangle** shape, draw two separate rectangles covering the bottom panels of the box.

   ![Two black rectangles drawn over the bottom panels of the box template](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_d355f9cd68.png)

2. Open the **Layers** panel at the top right. Hold **Shift** and select both new rectangles along with the template shape, then click **Combine** at the bottom of the panel and choose **Weld**.

   ![The Combine menu open on the Weld option, with both Square layers selected](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_1ca74fadd9.png)

   Weld merges the selected shapes into one and **can't be undone**, so make sure the rectangles are positioned before you weld.

3. Add a **line** from the Shapes menu between the two new rectangles, and weld that to the shape as well.

   ![The welded box outline with a line being added between the two rectangles](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_20fbf68d78.png)

4. Your outline is now a single closed shape with the template's fold lines still on top of it.

   ![The finished box outline with its dotted score lines](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_c9794b99ac.png)

5. Select all the fold lines the same way — **Shift** and click — then press **Attach**.

   ![All layers selected with the Attach button in the Layers panel toolbar](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_49ba314c4c.png)

   **Attach** is what keeps the fold lines in position relative to the outline. Skip it and Design Space will rearrange them anywhere it likes on the mat.

## 4. Cut it

The screenshots below use copy paper, but cut yours from **cardstock** — the box won't hold its shape in copy paper.

1. Press **Make** at the top right, then choose **On Mat** and confirm the mat size matches the mat you're holding — **12 in × 12 in** here.

   ![Design Space asking how the material will load, with On Mat and Without Mat options](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_130e3c6bf4.png)

2. Wait for Design Space to connect to the machine.

   ![Design Space waiting to connect to the Explore 4 over Bluetooth](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_8d5e2eec5d.png)

3. Set the base material to the matching **cardstock** option.

   ![The Set Base Material step showing the cardstock options](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_f9d45b53e5.png)

4. Load the tools Design Space asks for — the fine-point blade in **Clamp B**, nothing in **Clamp A**.

   ![The Load tools and material step showing which tool goes in which clamp](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_ef51f9d456.png)

5. Stick the cardstock to the mat, seat the mat inside the machine's material guides, and press **Load**.

   ![Cardstock attached to the cutting mat, ready to load](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_db4fdaff84.jpeg)

6. Press **Go**.

   ![The finished cut on the mat](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_1373099167.jpeg)

**Cut this shape twice** — you need two identical pieces to make one box.

## 5. Fold and assemble

Fold along the score lines on both pieces.

![The cut piece with the fold lines marked](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_7049bb50ed.jpeg)

Lay the two pieces side by side:

![The two cut pieces laid side by side](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_27e3c6bf6c.jpeg)

Tape the inner tab of the right-hand piece to the body of the left-hand piece:

![Taping the inner tab of the right piece to the body of the left piece](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_e73de8537c.jpeg)

The tab on the outside left wraps around and tapes to the body of the right-hand piece:

![The outer left tab wrapped around and taped to the right piece](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_0daeeb0968.jpeg)

Tape the two horizontal bottom flaps together:

![Taping the horizontal bottom flaps together](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_8f00a15d61.jpeg)

Then the vertical bottom flaps:

![Taping the vertical bottom flaps together](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_1c69178011.jpeg)

Finally, tuck the protruding ends of the handles into the holes on the top side flaps.

![The finished gable box](../../assets/images/cricut_explore_4_ass_688c0867de.jpeg)

## 6. Clean up

Follow [Finishing up](/docs/cricut/cricut-explore-4/#finishing-up) in the manual.
