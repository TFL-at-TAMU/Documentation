title: Laser Safety Certification
---

Some machines in the lab use lasers powerful enough to injure you (Class 3R, 3B, or 4), and Texas A&M requires **laser safety certification** before you operate one. In the Fab Lab that currently means the [Laser Cutter (Glowforge Pro)](/docs/laser-cutter/laser-cutter-glowforge-pro/) — its manual carries the red certification callout. Eye-safe Class 1/2 lasers, like the ones in the [3D scanner](/docs/3d-scanner/3d-scanner-creality-raptor-pro/), don't require certification.

The training is an online course run by TAMU Environmental Health & Safety. Here's how to get certified:

1. Go to the **[EHSA portal](https://ehsa.tamu.edu/ehsa/)** and click **"EHSA Online Training Courses"**.
2. Fill in your information:
   - **Email:** your TAMU email address
   - **Department:** Engineering
   - **Start Date:** today's date
   - **Worker Type:** Faculty, Staff, & Student (Non-Veterinary Hospital)
   - **Supervisor:** Deere, Paul
3. Complete the **LASER training course**.
4. Submit your certification through the [Fab Lab certification form](https://forms.gle/mkkrwYoRNVzxxLiCA) — **use your TAMU email address**.

Keep a copy of your certificate on hand — staff may ask to see it before you run a laser job.
